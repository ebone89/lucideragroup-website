SERVICE 01 / /CONSULTING

IT consulting and planning

Plain-English advice on what you have, what you need, and what to buy, with a written plan you can follow.

We look at your current equipment, software, and budget, then recommend a sensible order of work. It suits small businesses, offices, and organizations that want a second opinion before they spend money. You get a clear plan with priorities, not a pile of jargon.

SERVICE 02 / /TROUBLESHOOTING

Troubleshooting and repair

Computers, printers, email, Wi-Fi, and accounts that stopped working.

Bring us the slow laptop, the printer that will not connect, or the email account that keeps locking you out. We find the cause, fix it, and tell you what happened so it is less likely to happen again. Individuals and families are as welcome as offices.

SERVICE 03 / /MANAGED-IT

Systems administration and managed IT

Ongoing care for your computers, accounts, backups, and updates.

We keep your systems patched, your backups running, and your user accounts in order, and you have one place to send it when something breaks. It works well for small businesses, nonprofits, and churches without a full time IT person.

SERVICE 04 / /NETWORKS

Network and infrastructure build-outs

Wired and wireless networks designed, installed, and documented.

We plan and set up routers, switches, Wi-Fi coverage, cabling, and the equipment behind them for offices, buildings, and campuses. Every job ends with documentation so the next person can understand what was built.

SERVICE 05 / /WEBSITES

Website builds and hosting

Simple, fast websites for businesses, organizations, and individuals, with hosting if you want it.

We design and build the site, set up the domain and email, and host it or hand it off to you. Static sites are our default because they are quick, inexpensive to run, and hard to break. You can edit them yourself or leave the updates to us.

SERVICE 06 / /SECURITY

Digital security and privacy hardening

Protecting accounts, devices, and personal information, including doxxing protection for at-risk people.

We secure email and social accounts, set up password managers and two step sign-in, harden phones and computers, and reduce the personal information that can be found online. For people at risk of harassment or doxxing, we review what is exposed, help remove it where possible, and put protections in place.

SERVICE 07 / /ELECTION-SECURITY

Campaign and election security

Account security, phishing defense, and device hardening for campaign staff and committees of any party.

Campaigns and committees are targets for phishing, account takeovers, and leaked data. We lock down staff and volunteer accounts, train people to spot phishing, and harden the laptops and phones they use. This service is offered to any campaign or committee, regardless of party or position.

SERVICE 08 / /DEEPFAKE-ANALYSIS

Deepfake and manipulated media analysis

Technical review of images, audio, and video that may have been altered or synthesized.

If a clip, photo, or recording looks wrong, we examine it and report what we can and cannot say about its authenticity. We explain our method and our confidence in plain terms. This is useful for organizations, campaigns, and individuals who need to respond to questionable media.

SERVICE 09 / /TRAINING

Digital literacy workshops and training

Practical sessions on safe, confident technology use for staff, volunteers, and members.

We teach groups how to spot scams, use passwords and two step sign-in, protect their privacy, and handle everyday tools. Sessions are plain and hands-on, and can be held on site or online for businesses, churches, nonprofits, and community groups.
