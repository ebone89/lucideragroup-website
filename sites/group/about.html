ABOUT THE GROUP

Three companies, one standard

Lucid Era Group is based in Palatka, Florida. It holds three independent companies under one name.

// WHO RUNS IT

================================================================================================================

Lucid Era Group is run by Ethan Bradley in Palatka, Florida. He runs the group and each of its three companies. The same email reaches all of them: ethan@lucideragroup.com.

// WHAT EACH COMPANY DOES

================================================================================================================
FILE 01 / TECHNOLOGIES

Lucid Era Technologies

Lucid Era Technologies is an IT company. It handles consulting, repair, managed IT, network build-outs, websites and hosting, and digital security. It serves small businesses, families, nonprofits, churches, and political committees, and it works the same way for all of them.

FILE 02 / STRATEGIES

Lucid Era Strategies

Lucid Era Strategies is a political consulting and research company. It offers data analysis, surveys and polling, focus groups, voter file data, campaign strategy, and public records research. It serves progressive campaigns, committees, issue groups, and nonprofits. Its client work is confidential.

FILE 03 / NEWSROOM

Lucid Era Media

Lucid Era Media is the group's newsroom. It produces investigations, podcast and video work, and commentary. It is openly progressive, and its reporting is sourced and documented. Commentary is labeled as commentary.

// THE SEPARATION POLICY

================================================================================================================

The companies share an owner. They do not share clients, files, or editorial decisions.

Lucid Era Media does not report on anyone who is a client of Lucid Era Group, including clients of Lucid Era Technologies and Lucid Era Strategies. That covers anyone the group works with directly as a client, in any company. Where any connection to a subject exists, we disclose it.

Each company has its own website and its own contact page. Client work done by Lucid Era Technologies or Lucid Era Strategies stays private and does not appear in the newsroom. The newsroom's own standards and disclosures are published on its site.
