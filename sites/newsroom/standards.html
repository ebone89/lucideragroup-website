STANDARDS AND DISCLOSURE

We show our work.

Lucid Era Media is an independent accountability newsroom based in Putnam County, Florida. Lucid Era Investigations is our reporting section. These are the rules we hold ourselves to.

// OUR PRINCIPLES

================================================================================================================
PRINCIPLE 01

Public sources only

Every factual claim is sourced from public records, government filings, or documented public statements. No anonymous allegations without corroboration.

PRINCIPLE 02

Show your work

Methodology is documented and defensible. We cite sources, explain our process, and welcome scrutiny of our findings.

PRINCIPLE 03

No advertising

Lucid Era Media carries no paid advertising and accepts no money from the people or entities we cover. Independence is non-negotiable.

PRINCIPLE 04

Community first

Our coverage puts the perspectives of working people, renters, and underserved communities ahead of institutional PR.

// REPORTING VERSUS COMMENTARY

================================================================================================================
LABEL: REPORTING

What the record shows

Reporting is sourced and documented. Each claim is tagged Verified (primary source document), Reported (credible news coverage), or Inferred (a conclusion from verified facts we have not independently confirmed).

LABEL: COMMENTARY

What we think about it

Commentary is opinion, written from the openly progressive values on our what we stand for page. It is labeled as commentary at the top, every time.

// CORRECTIONS

================================================================================================================

When we get something wrong, we fix it and say so. Corrections are noted on the piece itself, with the date and what changed. We do not quietly edit a mistake away.

To report an error, email ethan@lucideragroup.com and put the article title in the subject line. Tell us what is wrong and, if you can, point us to the record that shows it.

// THE CLIENT FIREWALL

================================================================================================================
MEMO: FIREWALLLucid Era Media does not report on anyone who is a client of Lucid Era Group, including clients of Lucid Era Technologies and Lucid Era Strategies. Where any connection to a subject exists, we disclose it.

Lucid Era Media is one of three companies under Lucid Era Group. The other two, Lucid Era Technologies and Lucid Era Strategies, take paying clients. This newsroom does not cover them, and their clients have no say in what we publish.
