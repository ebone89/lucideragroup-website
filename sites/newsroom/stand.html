What we stand for

Lucid Era Media is openly progressive. We would rather tell you that up front than pretend to have no point of view.

These values shape what we choose to cover and what we say in commentary. They do not change the facts. Our reporting is sourced and documented whether or not the facts are convenient for our side.

// OUR VALUES

================================================================================================================
FILE V-01DECLASSIFIED

People-first populism

Government and the economy should answer to the people who live and work here, not to the wealthiest few. We cover power from the point of view of the people it lands on.

FILE V-02DECLASSIFIED

Workers' rights

Fair pay, safe jobs, and the freedom to organize. Working people built this county, and they deserve a real say in the decisions that shape their jobs and their towns.

FILE V-03DECLASSIFIED

Environmental protection

Clean water, clean air, and protected land are shared public goods. In Florida that means the aquifer, the springs, and the St. Johns River. They are not a giveaway to whoever asks first.

FILE V-04DECLASSIFIED

Anti-racism

Racism is built into a lot of how land, money, and policing have been handled, here and everywhere. We name it when the record shows it, and we listen to the communities it has hurt.

FILE V-05DECLASSIFIED

Anti-corruption

Public office is a public trust. We follow the money, read the disclosures, and ask who knew what and when. Conflicts of interest belong in the open.

FILE V-06DECLASSIFIED

Anti-greed

Profit is not a reason to drain public resources or push costs onto ordinary people. When a deal is good for a few and paid for by everyone else, we say so.

FILE V-07DECLASSIFIED

Collective responsibility over individualism

We do better when we look out for each other. Public schools, public health, public water, and public records only work when we treat them as ours.

// OUR POSITIONS ON TECHNOLOGY

================================================================================================================
FILE T-01DECLASSIFIED

Privacy rights

Information about you should belong to you. We support strong limits on surveillance, data brokers, and the quiet collection of personal data.

FILE T-02DECLASSIFIED

Right to repair and digital ownership

If you bought it, you own it. People should be able to fix their own devices and equipment, get the parts and manuals to do it, and keep the digital things they paid for.

FILE T-03DECLASSIFIED

Tech regulation that works for people

We support tech regulation that makes technology safer and more beneficial for everyone, written in public and enforced, not left to the companies selling it.

FILE T-04DECLASSIFIED

Fiber internet for everyone

Fast, reliable internet is basic infrastructure, like roads and power. Rural counties like Putnam should not be left behind.

FILE T-05DECLASSIFIED

Access to technology

Devices, connections, and the skills to use them safely should be within reach for every household, school, and library, not just the ones that can easily pay.

FILE T-06DECLASSIFIED

Responsible use of technology

New technology should be weighed by what it costs the people around it, not just what it earns. That includes AI and the data centers built to run it, and the water, power, and land they use.

// HOW THIS FITS WITH OUR REPORTING

================================================================================================================

Values are where we stand. Standards are how we keep the reporting honest anyway: public sources, documented methods, labels on commentary, and corrections when we get something wrong.
